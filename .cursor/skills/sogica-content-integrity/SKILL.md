# SOGICA Content Integrity

## Non-negotiable rule

Never invent business facts.

Do not invent:
- projects;
- project names;
- clients or partners;
- budgets;
- contract values;
- dates;
- durations;
- locations;
- team size;
- employee count;
- revenue;
- certificates;
- awards;
- testimonials;
- project metrics;
- equipment;
- legal identifiers;
- phone numbers or email addresses;
- claims such as "leader", "number one", "largest", or "best" unless explicitly sourced and approved.

If content is missing, use a neutral CMS placeholder state or leave the field empty. Do not publish lorem ipsum on production pages.

## Writing tone

- Professional, factual, concise, confident without exaggeration.
- Prefer concrete expertise and project evidence over generic slogans.
- French copy must read as natural professional French, not translated AI copy.
- English copy should preserve meaning without adding claims.
- Do not auto-translate names, legal identifiers, acronyms, or official client names when they should remain unchanged.

## Media integrity

- Prefer media explicitly supplied for SOGICA.
- Do not fetch random project images and imply they are SOGICA work.
- Do not attach a client logo unless provided or explicitly approved.
